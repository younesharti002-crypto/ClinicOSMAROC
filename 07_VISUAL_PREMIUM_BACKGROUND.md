# ClinicOS — Premium Background Pass

This visual pass adds a subtle premium medical background to the authenticated application shell.

## Visual intent

- abstract medical atmosphere, not stock photography
- clinic-first identity remains dominant
- light navy/teal geometry with a restrained ECG motif
- fixed background with a readability overlay
- glass-like header treatment
- cards and forms remain visually clear and high contrast

## Safety

This is presentation-only. It does not change:
- medical workflows
- RBAC
- tenant isolation
- clinical projections
- billing or cash rules
- database schema
- security behavior
